# University of Detroit Mercy — Brand Story Analysis

## What They're Saying
You have spoken with the warm, proud, and welcoming voice of a trusted neighbor who cannot wait to show visitors around their hometown. Through your messaging, you are telling prospective students and families that a transformative Catholic education in the Jesuit and Mercy traditions isn't a distant, intimidating luxury—it is an accessible, community-driven partnership deeply connected to the heartbeat of a legendary city. 

## The Words That Work
*   **"Have a weekday free?"**  
    This is wonderfully conversational, disarming, and low-pressure. Instead of using stiff, institutional jargon to command a campus visit, this phrase sounds like an invitation from a friendly peer, instantly lowering the barrier to engagement for busy families.
*   **"An Education You Can Afford" / "We specialize in creating the most affordable financial aid package..."**  
    Rather than burying the cost conversation or wrapping it in clinical terms like "financial aid leveraging," you address affordability with refreshing clarity and empathy. Using the word "specialize" is brilliant—it frames the university not just as an assessor of finances, but as an active partner and advocate for the family’s peace of mind.
*   **"You're going to love our location."**  
    This is a remarkably confident and enthusiastic host speaking. By leading with this bold, positive assertion, you reframe the campus’s Detroit setting as an undeniable asset and a source of adventure, setting an expectant, exciting tone for the student experience.
*   **"Meet Your Tour Guides"**  
    Placing this link alongside practical logistics like maps and directions is a beautiful touch. It signals to prospective students that they aren't just visiting a collection of brick-and-mortar buildings; they are being welcomed by real, friendly peers who are excited to share their lives.

## The Visual Story
*   **The City as an Extension of Campus:** Your choice to feature video content like *"Happy Birthday, Detroit!"* alongside iconic local imagery (like the Detroit Red Wings stadium) is incredibly powerful. It visually communicates that Detroit Mercy does not exist in a bubble; instead, it invites students to claim a legendary American city as their extended classroom, living room, and playground. 
*   **Joyful, Collaborative Discovery:** The photograph of the two students smiling over a petri dish in the lab is a masterclass in authentic visual storytelling. Instead of the cliché "lone scientist" staring intensely into a microscope, this image captures joy, human connection, and shared discovery—perfectly reflecting the relational, community-focused nature of a Mercy and Jesuit education.
*   **Grounded Openness:** Throughout the page, the imagery balances the energy of the city with peaceful, sun-dappled lawns, tree-lined paths, and open spaces. This visual balance reassures parents and students alike that they will enjoy the best of both worlds: a vibrant urban adventure and a safe, classic collegiate sanctuary where they can ground themselves.

## What to Borrow
*   **Attach Financial Promises Directly to Engagement Milestones**  
    You don't wait for families to find the financial aid page to ease their affordability anxieties; you boldly feature the "$4,000 Visit Grant" right on the freshman visit card. Other Catholic school communicators should borrow this strategy of transparently showing how simple engagement steps directly translate into tangible, supportive tuition relief.
*   **Make Your City Your Co-Author**  
    Rather than treating an urban location as a detail to explain away, you celebrate it as a core pillar of your identity with content like "Discover Detroit." Other institutions in metropolitan areas should adopt this proud stance, confidently claiming their cities as vital components of their student experience, career networking, and civic mission.
*   **Humanize the Visit Logistics Page**  
    By grouping standard visit links with "Meet Your Tour Guides" and "Meet Your Admission Counselor," you humanize what is typically a very dry, functional area of a website. Other schools should borrow this approach to remind visiting families of the personal care (*personalis cura*) awaiting them the moment they step onto campus.

## The One Thing
Detroit Mercy brilliantly weaves civic pride and unpretentious warmth together, showing families that a values-driven, top-tier education is both deeply supportive and thrillingly connected to the energy of a great city.
