# St. Thomas University — Brand Story Analysis

## What They're Saying
St. Thomas University speaks directly to ambitious, community-minded students with a voice that is vibrant, warm, and deeply hospitable. They are telling a story of immediate belonging, where prospective students aren't just names in a database, but welcomed guests who are invited to experience the sunny, high-energy rhythm of campus life firsthand. It’s a message that says, "We are excited to meet you, we have prepared a place for you, and we want to invest in your future from day one."

## The Words That Work
*   **"personalized visits of our beautiful campus, accompanied by a meeting with an admissions counselor"**  
    This shifts the narrative from a generic group tour to an individualized, VIP experience. It signals to families that STU values the unique journey and goals of every single student.
*   **"$4,000 Campus Visit Award"**  
    This is an incredibly powerful way to demonstrate tangible, generous investment in the student before they even enroll. It reframes a simple campus visit not just as an admissions step, but as a valuable financial opportunity for families.
*   **"visits typically last 1 hour and are provided by golf cart"**  
    This copy is practical, comforting, and sounds genuinely fun. It sets clear, low-stress expectations while promising a comfortable, breezy experience under the warm Florida sun.
*   **"Experience campus in 360° without leaving home!"**  
    This language is highly welcoming and accessible. It recognizes that distance shouldn't be a barrier to discovering the STU community, extending an open invitation to global audiences.
*   **"...please contact Madelyn Caraballo, guest relations coordinator..."**  
    Putting a real name, specific title, and direct contact details right there on the page breaks down the cold wall of institutional bureaucracy. It instantly replaces a standard department contact with personal, relational hospitality.

## The Visual Story
*   **The Energy of the Hero Image:** The prominent hero photo featuring a diverse, joyful group of students leaping in front of the giant "STU" letters is pure energy. It tells a visual story of a dynamic, enthusiastic, and highly collaborative student body, instantly signaling to prospective students that they will find their crew and their joy here.
*   **The Warmth of the Weather Widget:** Integrating a live local weather forecast directly on the visit page is a brilliant, subtle touch of Miami hospitality. It doesn't just help families pack the right clothes; it visually reinforces the desirable, sun-drenched location of the campus, transforming a logistical detail into an enticing lifestyle feature.
*   **Clear, Inviting Tour Previews:** The side-by-side virtual tour blocks act as open windows into the STU experience. By showcasing a bright "360° Virtual Campus Tour" alongside a real student residence video ("Murphy Double Room"), the visuals demystify college life and invite students to actively project themselves into these spaces.

## What to Borrow

*   **Roll Out the Financial Red Carpet (The Visit Scholarship)**  
    STU’s "$4,000 Campus Visit Award" is a masterclass in demonstrating how much you value a student’s presence. Consider how you can attach tangible institutional aid to physical or virtual visits to honor a family’s time investment and immediately establish a culture of mutual commitment.
*   **Bring the Environment to Them (The Local Weather Widget)**  
    The inclusion of a 3-day weather forecast is incredibly smart and easy to implement. Whether your school is in a sunny, historic, snowy, or coastal location, sharing a slice of your daily atmosphere builds anticipation and helps out-of-town families feel cared for before they even arrive.
*   **Ditch the Generic Forms for a Real Human Connection**  
    By naming their Guest Relations Coordinator directly with a personal email and text number, STU moves from transactional to relational. Catholic higher education thrives on personalism, and putting a real, friendly face at the front door of your visit page is something every campus can—and should—emulate.

## The One Thing
St. Thomas University beautifully blends high-energy Florida sunshine with deeply personal, relational hospitality to make every prospective student feel valued before they even step foot on campus.
