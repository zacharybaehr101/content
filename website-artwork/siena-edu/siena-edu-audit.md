# Siena — Brand Story Analysis

## What They're Saying
Siena presents itself as a warm, high-achieving, and deeply relational family where rigorous career preparation and Franciscan values live in perfect, effortless harmony. You are speaking directly to driven, heart-centered prospective students who want to succeed wildly in their careers without losing their souls or their sense of community along the way. It’s a beautiful message of academic ambition wrapped in an embrace of radical hospitality.

## The Words That Work
*   **"Rankings are cool, but Saints aren't numbers."**  
    This is a masterclass in subverting the typical higher-ed brag sheet. It brilliantly acknowledges your stellar data points while immediately pivoting to what actually matters—individual human dignity and personal growth—which is the absolute heart of the Franciscan tradition.
*   **"Whoever comes to you, friend or foe... receive them with kindness." — St. Francis**  
    Placing this radical quote so prominently on your site signals that community at Siena isn’t just a marketing buzzword; it’s a spiritual commitment to welcoming others. It sets a beautifully disarming, hospitable tone for any prospective student wondering if they will truly belong.
*   **"Liberal arts is the way to go, period."**  
    This phrase is bold, conversational, and completely devoid of stuffy academic jargon. It sounds exactly like a passionate, trusted professor giving real advice over coffee, instantly humanizing your academic experience.
*   **"Get in the game."**  
    Instead of dryly talking about "experiential learning," this active, energetic phrase invites students to be active participants in their education rather than mere spectators. It perfectly sets up your stories about hands-on internships, research, and service.
*   **"Every Saint has a story..."**  
    By turning your school identity ("Saints") into an active narrative lens, this phrase reinforces that every student’s journey is uniquely valued. It builds an immediate sense of shared identity and belonging before a student even steps onto campus.

## The Visual Story
*   **The Joy of Authentic Connection:** Across almost every image—whether it’s students collaborating in a lab, laughing together on the quad with a giant Siena flag, or gathered around a campus firepit—there is an overwhelming sense of joy and genuine connection. Your photos don't look staged or corporate; they feel like captured moments of an authentic, lived community.
*   **Radiant, Confident Brand Colors:** Your bold use of deep forest green and warm, golden yellow creates a visual language that feels both deeply rooted/prestigious and incredibly welcoming and bright. The yellow doesn't just function as a secondary color; it floods your pages with a sunlit, optimistic energy that beautifully reflects your Franciscan spirit of joy.
*   **The Balance of Rigor and Heart:** The visual narrative masterfully pairs high-level achievement with tender, personal care. Seeing photos of high-tech scientific research side-by-side with images of service projects, community-building, and campus ministry shows prospective families that academic ambition and a generous spirit are two sides of the same coin at Siena.

## What to Borrow
*   **Deploy Your Mascot as a Shared Way of Being.** You don’t just use "Saints" as an athletic moniker; you weave it into the very fabric of your copy ("Saints aren't numbers," "Saints around the world," "How to live like a Saint"). Other Catholic school communicators should look for ways to elevate their school moniker from a sports brand to a shared identity, helping prospective students picture themselves as part of a distinct, living tradition.
*   **Translate Financial Value into Welcoming Warmth.** You talk about affordability and financial outcomes with immense clarity and human touch—using clear, direct metrics alongside reassuring copy. Other schools can learn from how you frame financial aid as a mutual investment in the student's future, reinforcing that they are wanted and valued rather than just showing them a sterile calculator.
*   **Differentiate the Visit Journey.** Rather than a one-size-fits-all "Schedule a Visit" button, you map out a delightful, customized menu of visits—from virtual college coach sessions and on-campus interviews to student-led tours and preview days. Other colleges can borrow this highly hospitable approach to meet students exactly where they are in their college search process.

## The One Thing
Siena brilliantly proves that a college can showcase world-class career outcomes and top-tier job placement rates while remaining fiercely and beautifully committed to a Franciscan culture of radical hospitality, kindness, and personal belonging.
